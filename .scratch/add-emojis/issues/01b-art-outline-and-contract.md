# 01b - Art: sticker outline, tone mapping, layer cap

**What to build:** Three amendments to the art package that the design review asked for after
ticket 01 started. Rules: [../RULES.md](../RULES.md). Spec D1, D2, D3.

**Blocked by:** [01 - Emoji art package](01-emoji-art-package.md)

**Status:** done

## Areas

`packages/caption-emoji/**` only.

## Deliverables

1. **`outline` per design.** The silhouette dilated outward by a fixed width on the 64 grid, round
   joins, as one non-zero fill path (stroke the silhouette at twice the width, union with the
   silhouette, simplify). Width: start at 2.2 grid units (the owner's prototype draws a 1.5 px white
   outline around a 112 px emoji plus a hard shadow; at caption sizes a slightly heavier band reads
   better); export the width as `CAPTION_EMOJI_OUTLINE_GRID`. The renderers draw `outline` in white
   under the colour layers and give that one piece a hard offset shadow. A shape's shadow follows
   only its fill in both renderers, which is why the outline must be a fill and not a stroke.
   `CaptionEmojiArt` becomes `{ id, layers, silhouette, outline }`.
2. **Drawing box.** Export `CAPTION_EMOJI_GRID = 64` and `CAPTION_EMOJI_BOX`: the one box, in grid
   units, that contains every design's `outline` (expected: the grid grown by the outline width,
   plus any stroke reach beyond the grid; measure it over all designs and record the result). Every
   emoji piece will be drawn inside this same box so libass scales all layers of an emoji about one
   point.
3. **Layer cap.** No design may need more than 22 colour layers (the renderers cap an emoji at 24
   pieces: outline, and at most 22 layers, leaving one spare). Report the distribution; if any
   design exceeds 22, merge further or simplify that design and say which.
4. **`emojiRefForGlyph(glyph): { id; tone? } | null`** replacing `emojiIdForGlyph` if that is what
   ticket 01 shipped: a skin-tone modifier becomes `tone` 1 to 5 when the design has tones (and is
   ignored when it does not); U+FE0F dropped; a gendered ZWJ sequence falls back to the neutral
   design. Only `kind: "single"` designs are returned (phrase badges are picker-only).
   Add `CAPTION_EMOJI_AI_GLYPHS`: the single designs' glyphs in a stable order, for the AI prompt.
5. Extend the fidelity script and the contact sheet to show the sticker look (white outline plus a
   hard offset shadow) for the sample designs, on a dark and a light background.

### Public API (changes)

  `CAPTION_EMOJI_OUTLINE_GRID = 2.2`, `CAPTION_EMOJI_BOX = { x: -2.3, y: -2.3, width: 68.6, height: 68.6 }`
  (frozen; centred on the grid).
- `CaptionEmojiArt` is `{ id, layers, silhouette, outline }`.
- `emojiRefForGlyph(glyph: string): CaptionEmojiRef | null` with
  `CaptionEmojiRef = { readonly id: string; readonly tone?: 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 }` replaces
  `emojiIdForGlyph` (it had no consumers).
- `CAPTION_EMOJI_AI_GLYPHS: readonly string[]`: the 1,008 single designs' glyphs in catalog order,
  frozen.

### How

- Outline: silhouette dilated by 2.2 + 1 (Skia round stroke united with the shape), eroded back by
  1 (the shape minus a round band of its own edge), then only outer contours kept. The closing
  rounds concave corners and fills notches under 6.4 units wide.
- Layer cap: two compiler changes, no art edits. (1) A layer is dropped when, grown by 0.25 grid
  units with its holes filled, opaque layers above still cover it (layers drawn at every tone only
  count as cover): 214 layers in 133 designs, for example 12 in each astronaut (a whole face drawn
  under the helmet), 5 in each factory worker. (2) Two layers of one colour and alpha unite when
  either can move to the other's place without passing over a layer it overlaps (ticket 01 only
  moved the upper one down to the nearest same-colour layer). The build fails above 22.
- Drawing box: libass sizes a drawing by all its points, control points included. Source curves
  had control points out to -6 (crescent, the night phrases). The writer now splits a cubic in
  half (exactly) until its control points lie in the box. The build fails if any point of any
  layer, silhouette or outline falls outside it. Measured reach after the split: -2.3 to 66.2 on
  both axes; on-curve outline points -2.2 to 66.2.
- Outline coordinates use 1 decimal (error at most 0.05 grid units, a third of a pixel on the
  largest sticker, and the art covers its inner edge). Layers and silhouette keep 2.
- Files: `compile.ts` was split. Path writing and contour helpers moved to `scripts/path-data.ts`,
  dilation and outline to `scripts/outline.ts`. New: `scripts/outline-check.ts`, `src/geometry.ts`.

### Bug found by looking and fixed

Skia's stroker draws a contour's inner side wrongly when the half-width is more than twice the
curve's radius. The inner offset flips outward and leaves a ring of winding 0. Example: the tiny
sun rays (radius 1.1) in a 3.2 band. The first outline build had holes round the rays in 5 designs
(sun-behind-large-cloud, police-car-light, the three raising-hand designs), and the outline check
found them as missing band pixels. Keeping only outer contours removes such rings, because they
are always holes.

### Deviations

- The outline fills the silhouette's holes (spec: "the silhouette dilated"). A kept hole shows the
  hard shadow inside the emoji (a snowflake gets six shadow specks), and keeping holes also kept
  Skia's false rings. Without the fill, 202 holes remained in 1,408 designs, for example the
  snowflake, the roller-coaster slots and the inside of the 100.
- A closing radius of 1 grid unit was added so narrow notches between parts (spider legs,
  musical staff lines) fill, with no sliver between them.
- `CAPTION_EMOJI_BOX` is 0.1 wider than the outline reach on each side. Curve splitting needs
  that margin: an arc's control points cannot all sit exactly on its extreme.
- The geometry constants are exported from both entry points (one module, `src/geometry.ts`), so
  code that holds only the art types and code that holds only the index both have them.
