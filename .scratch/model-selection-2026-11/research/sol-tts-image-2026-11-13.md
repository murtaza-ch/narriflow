# Narriflow TTS and image replacement research

Research date: **2026-11-13**. All pricing documents cited below were opened or fetched during this research today. Prices are provider-direct API prices, in USD unless marked INR. No repository files were edited. No paid inference, listening tests, or image generations were run.

`NV` means not verified in the official sources inspected, not that the capability does not exist. A language omitted from an official support list is marked **not listed**, rather than called technically impossible. Recommendations are engineering judgments from the documented contracts and dated evaluations; they are not claims that I listened to the models.

## Decisions in brief

- OpenAI's official replacement for `gpt-4o-mini-tts` is **`gpt-realtime-2.1-mini` through the Realtime API**, not a replacement model ID on `/v1/audio/speech`. However, **`gpt-audio-1.5` is available over ordinary HTTP on `/v1/chat/completions`**. The latter is an audio-generating chat model, with a different request/response contract. [OpenAI TTS migration](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/text-to-speech), [GPT-Audio-1.5](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-audio-1.5).
- For the current one-script/one-POST dubbing job, my first default audition is **Inworld Realtime TTS-2 through its OpenAI-compatible speech endpoint**. It documents a 4,000-character input, MP3, all eight target languages, and original-speaker cloning. **Speechify Simba 3.5** is the cheaper challenger with explicit Hindi and Urdu and a 20,000-character streaming HTTP endpoint. **Eleven v4** is the premium HTTP candidate. Cartesia Sonic 3.6 deserves an equal-priority Hindi/Hinglish/Urdu audition. Details and the ranking are at the end.
- Sarvam Bulbul currently **does not list Urdu TTS**. Its Urdu support for transcription/translation is a different product. Gemini 3.8 TTS and xAI TTS also omit Urdu from their current explicit TTS support tables. [Sarvam model-specific language matrix](https://docs.sarvam.ai/api/getting-started/models), [Gemini TTS languages](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/speech-generation#languages), [xAI TTS languages](https://docs.x.ai/developers/model-capabilities/audio/text-to-speech).
- OpenAI officially recommends **GPT-Image-2.6 Flare or Sunburst** for `gpt-image-2`. For this text-free B-roll job, start with **Flare medium**; audition **Seedream 5.5 and Meta Muse Image** as cheaper alternatives, and **Sunburst high/max** for premium output. Do not assume AA's `max` scores describe `medium`. [OpenAI deprecations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/deprecations).
- Two time-sensitive exclusions: Hume's indexed official notice says TTS/EVI access ends **today, November 13 at 00:01 EST**. Midjourney does not offer a generally available official API. Neither is a replacement to build now. [Hume October 2 sunset notice](https://dev.hume.ai/voice/changelog), [Midjourney community rules](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32013696484109-Community-Guidelines). Hume's legacy URLs now redirect to Audio Decisions; the exact sunset notice was available in the official search index today, rather than on the redirected live page.

## Normalization and the actual job

At the requested **15 characters/second**, one generated hour is **54,000 characters**, and a 180-second clip is **2,700 characters**. Thus:

- Character-metered cost/hour = official USD/1M characters × **0.054**.
- Cost/180-second clip = cost/hour ÷ **20**.
- These are capacity-planning estimates. Character counts, pronunciation, speaking speed, pauses, script expansion, retries, and Unicode billing rules change the actual invoice.
- Token-metered audio uses the provider's documented audio-token rate, not an assumed character/token conversion. Input text and any generated transcript are additional charges where applicable.
- Subscription rates below require consuming the included capacity. A low advertised unit rate with unused monthly credits is not the effective price for a small workload.

For INR conversions only, the RBI page opened today displayed **INR 94.1997 per USD**, dated **November 13, 2026, 13:00 India time**, source FBIL. I divide INR prices by that rate; this is not a Sarvam USD quote and excludes tax/payment FX. [RBI exchange-rate display](https://www.rbi.org.in/?id=12656).

The existing image sizes are **1:1, 2:3, and 3:2**. `1024×1536` is **not 9:16**. Below, square means roughly 1MP, usually 1024². The OpenAI exact 9:16 example is **720×1280**, 0.922MP. Other providers' 1K aspect-ratio presets may use slightly different dimensions. This avoids silently pricing a 2MP portrait as a 1MP image.

### Hindi, Urdu, and code-mixing evidence

1. **Anand et al., “Preferences of a Voice-First Nation,” submitted April 23, 2026, Interspeech 2026.** SpeechArenaBench includes 5,357 native/symbolic/code-mixed sentences, ten Indic languages including Hindi and Urdu, and over 120K comparisons by 1,915 native raters. Its overall Bradley–Terry table reports Gemini 2.5 Pro TTS 1128.53±3, Eleven v3 1056.28±2, Sonic 3 1050.83±3, Bulbul v3 Beta 1021.91±3, Speech 2.8 HD 993.94±6, GPT-4o-mini TTS 942.76±4, Indic F5 805.75±3. **Model coverage varies by language**, so these are not seven-way Urdu-only scores. Gemini leads its code-mixed subset. This supports testing native scripts, symbols and mixed English separately; it does **not** establish that Gemini 3.8 officially supports Urdu or rank today's Sonic 3.6/v4/Simba 3.5. [Paper and tables 3–4](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.21481v1), [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/SpeechArenaBench).
2. **“Domain-Specific Evaluation of Text-to-Speech Systems,” submitted August 3, 2026.** This Urdu study compares Gemini **2.5 Flash**, Edge `ur-PK-AsadNeural`, MMS-TTS and Indic-Parler over 960 audio pairs across formal, conversational, literary and emotional speech. It reports a MUSHRA-style 1–5 overall score of **3.040 for Gemini**, **2.947 for Edge**, with emotional speech the most difficult domain. Acoustic/reference-similarity metrics rank systems differently. It tests Edge's service, not Azure HD 2.5 or newer Gemini 3.8. Treat its score scale and speaker-reference design as study-specific. [Paper, especially sections 3 and 5](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.02235v1).
3. **Menta, “PSP: An Interpretable Per-Dimension Accent Benchmark for Indic TTS,” April 28, 2026.** Small Hindi/Telugu/Tamil pilots compare older Eleven v3, Sonic 3, Sarvam Bulbul and Indic Parler. Hindi pilot WER is 0.006 for Eleven, 0.025 for Cartesia; Sarvam leads the Hindi FAD metric at 211.8. It explicitly warns about small samples and defers formal MOS correlation. This is evidence that intelligibility and native accent/prosody measure different things, not proof of a current model winner. [Paper, tables V–VI and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.25476v1).

I found **no named independent paper/leaderboard comparing current v4, Sonic 3.6, Simba 3.5, Inworld TTS-2, Uplift, Fish Drama 3 and Gemini 3.8 on the same Hindi/Urdu/Hinglish set**. Uplift's “outperforms” language and providers' “native quality” claims are not used as research conclusions here.

### ElevenLabs Dubbing API and original-speaker cloning

This is a different product choice from sending an already translated script to TTS. Dubbing accepts source audio/video, performs translation/alignment and preserves speaker identity/background audio. The current docs describe **Dubbing v2 Alpha**, 90+ languages including Hindi and Urdu, cloning-strength control 0–10, and API input files up to **3GB**. App and Studio duration limits differ; the app's duration limit should not be silently quoted as the API's. It is asynchronous: submit a job, check completion, download the target audio. A 180-second completion SLA was **not verified**. API transcript editing/regeneration for v2 is Enterprise-only in the current docs. [Dubbing capabilities and caveats](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/dubbing).

At the published v2 meter, a 3-minute clip costs **$6.60**, versus roughly **$0.216** for v4 TTS under the requested character cadence. The extra spend buys a different workflow. Audition it where retaining the source speaker is a paid feature; do not hide this cost inside the ordinary translated-script voice option. Cloning-only TTS can also preserve a chosen identity while keeping Narriflow's translation pipeline, but requires collecting/retaining a reference and measuring cross-language likeness.

## Ranked recommendations and auditions

### Job 1: translated-script dubbing TTS

1. **Default: Inworld Realtime TTS-2**, through `POST /v1/audio/speech`. It preserves the current binary MP3, one-request workflow for the assumed 2,700-character clip, documents Hindi and Urdu, and has AA **1268±13 as retrieved Nov 13**. Budget **$1.35/generated hour PAYG**, or roughly **$0.0675/180-second clip**, before retries. A script exceeding **4,000 characters** needs explicit handling. Urdu is Tier 2, so this is a candidate default pending native-speaker acceptance, not a claim of Urdu parity with Hindi. Verify training/retention settings before sending customer content.
2. **Cheaper alternative: Speechify Simba 3.5 on `/v1/audio/stream`**. Explicit all-eight language support and **20K** spoken-character HTTP input fit this job. AA **1294±17 on Nov 13** makes it a serious price/quality challenger, not just a low-cost control. Budget the **regular $0.81/hour Starter usage rate**, not only today's $0.54 launch rate. The $10 monthly plan and extra included usage change low-volume effective cost. **Production blocker to resolve: API-specific no-training/default retention/ZDR entitlement were not verified.** If native Urdu/Hinglish and contract checks pass, I would promote it over Inworld. For a lower-cost option within Inworld itself, TTS-2 Flash is $0.486/hour on Builder, with lower AA Elo and a monthly commitment.

Use v4 Turbo only if adopting its documented WebSocket workflow is acceptable. Do not pay a higher transport/integration cost merely for time-to-first-audio in an offline render job.
4. **Priority challenger: Cartesia Sonic 3.6**, AA **1294±13 on Nov 13**. It has the most directly relevant official documentation here for romanized Hindi/Hinglish and newly added Urdu, plus HTTP MP3. At Startup's included-capacity rate it is ~$2.12/hour. Confirm a 2,700–4,000-character call, choose a native Urdu voice, and process the training opt-out.
