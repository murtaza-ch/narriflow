# Competitor pricing — OpusClip and Vizard (gathered 2026-10-26)

Sources: https://www.opus.pro/pricing and https://vizard.ai/pricing (fetched 2026-10-26), plus
third-party summaries where the pages render prices as placeholders
(quso.ai, frameos.studio, usagepricing.com, layer3labs.io, aivideosignal.com, makerstack.co).
Conflicts are flagged, not resolved.

## Shared credit model

Both: **1 credit = 1 minute of uploaded source video**, charged at intake regardless of clip
count. Opus reportedly rounds partial minutes down, with sub-minute videos costing 1 credit.
Generation features (AI B-roll, voice-over, speech enhancement) are NOT charged in credits at
Opus — they are per-day/per-month count limits per plan.

## Takeaways for Narriflow

- Credits are sold as minutes of source video; the unit people compare is "credits/month at price".
- Free ≈ 60 credits/mo everywhere, watermark, 720p–1080p, 3-day retention.
- Entry paid ≈ $15–29/mo for 150–600 credits; annual ≈ 50% off.
- API/MCP is a Pro-level feature at Opus (not Business-only); Vizard gives API on all plans with rate limits.
- Generation features are count-limited per plan, not credit-metered, at Opus.
