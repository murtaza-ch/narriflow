# Credit pricing plan and enforcement review

Date: 2026-10-26. Status: proposal for owner and implementation review. Scope: this document only. No application, database, Stripe, environment, or other repository files were changed.

## Recommendation

Use credits. The existing Processing Usage ledger is already the right model for them. Keep `PricingTier`, Workspace Billing, intake reservations, the Workspace usage lock, probed-duration settlement, and durable operation receipts. This is a product-policy change and an enforcement cleanup, not a new wallet or billing system.

Launch with one credit equal to one started minute of source media, reserved once at intake and confirmed before the first speech-to-text admission. Use a UTC calendar month, no rollover, no top-ups, and separate generation counts. Keep the Free editor useful. Sell paid output quality, reusable branding, automation, integrations, and collaboration. Remove the generated-video promise until that product exists.

Fix duplicate-subscription admission and terminal-subscription seat reconciliation before changing prices. These affect real money independently of what a credit is called.

Proposed commercial defaults, requiring owner approval:

| Plan | Monthly USD | Annual total USD | Annual monthly equivalent | Credits each UTC month |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Free | 0 | 0 | 0 | 60 |
| Creator | 15 | 144 | 12 | 300 |
| Pro | 29 | 276 | 23 | 900 |
| Business | 49 | 468 | 39 | 900 shared |

Business includes its owner. Each additional Editor or Admin costs $5/month or $60/year; Viewers are free. Seats do not multiply credits. Annual subscriptions receive monthly allowances, not the entire year's credits upfront. The proposed paid allowances are deliberately below today's 600/1800/1800 minutes. There are no production customers to grandfather. These numbers are commercial recommendations, not a verified gross-margin calculation.

## 2. Bugs and gaps, ordered by severity

P1 means fix before accepting paid subscriptions under the new model. P2 means fix before promising the corresponding feature/limit. No P0 or confirmed live double charge was established.

### Top ten

1. **B01, P1, verified missing admission invariant: different Checkout keys create concurrent subscriptions.** `workspace-billing.service.ts:2466` reads Free before preparing Checkout; `:1058` deduplicates only `clientIdempotencyKey`; `:1065` creates a new attempt for another key; `:2594` creates a subscription Checkout with an attempt-specific provider key. `schema.prisma:1024` has no Workspace-level active-attempt constraint. Scenario: two tabs on Free use different UUIDs before either payment reconciles, complete both sessions, and Stripe has two subscriptions. Later conflict detection at `:2143` reports attention but does not prevent either charge. Fix one durable open Checkout slot per Billing Account, and verify provider subscription state before offering a new subscription. Test different-key concurrency, not just same-key replay.

2. **B02, P1, verified wrong recovery path: Free projection is mistaken for no live subscription.** After seven days of `past_due`, `:2198` sets pricingTier Free. `startCheckout:2470` permits a new subscription based on that tier, without retrieving provider subscription state. `unpaid` and `paused` produce the same problem. Scenario: owner clicks an upgrade after grace; old subscription later retries payment while the new subscription bills too. A recoverable provider subscription must route to the portal, independent of projected tier. A verified canceled/expired subscription may permit a new Checkout. Do not block all returning customers merely because a historical subscription ID exists.

3. **B03, P1, verified cancellation projection can be blocked by seat work.** `:2210` computes canceled -> Free, then `:2237` enters seat synchronization whenever the mapped price is Business, including canceled/unpaid/paused subscriptions. If member count changed, `:2253`, `:2276`, or `:2294` mutates the terminal subscription before `commitVerifiedState:2317`. Scenario: pending seat removal plus cancellation; Stripe rejects mutation of the canceled subscription; catch at `:2805` retains the previous paid projection and retries. Fix by never mutating seats on a terminal/non-billable subscription and committing verified access removal independently of optional seat convergence. Live provider rejection is a conditional part of this scenario, not a tested API result.

4. **B04, P1, verified collaboration leaks below Business.** `:2173` sets any active mapped paid tier to `status: active`; only Free paths consider non-owner members. Membership restrictions apply to additions, not use of existing roles. `workspace-access.ts:70` permits every capability held by an existing role on an active Workspace. Scenario: Business with five Editors changes to Pro; all five retain editing/processing/publishing, while the account no longer has the Business seat arrangement. Restrict a non-Business workspace with non-owner members until the owner upgrades or removes them; preserve owner recovery and downloads. Scheduled downgrade timing must be explicit.

5. **B05, P1, conditional money-correctness risk: full higher-tier allowance before upgrade payment.** Runbook `docs/runbooks/workspace-billing.md:67` prescribes `create_prorations`; `billing.service.ts:219` does not project paid-upgrade evidence, and reconciliation `:2173` grants the mapped active plan. Source allowance reads the current tier at `processing-usage.ts:274`. Scenario: an immediately applied portal upgrade creates deferred proration items; user consumes the larger allowance before paying the upgrade amount, then downgrades or fails payment. [Stripe's proration documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/prorations) says positive prorations need not be billed immediately. [Pending updates](https://docs.stripe.com/billing/subscriptions/pending-updates) document the explicit payment-dependent update mechanism. Verify the actual portal's payment behavior; require a test-mode paid-upgrade/SCA/failure trace before launch. Do not call every active subscription unpaid; the concern is newly expanded entitlement.

6. **B06, P1, verified tier changes hide branding and break exports.** `brand-ownership.ts:46` uses user ownership for personal Creator/Pro and Workspace ownership for personal Business. `brand-profile.service.ts:329`, `:375`, `:384` use that choice for lookup/defaults. `clip-export.service.ts:410` repeats it for Scene asset lookup. Billing tier transitions move neither owner nor default. Scenario: Creator creates a profile/font/image scene, upgrades the same personal Workspace to Business, then cannot list/apply the profile and gets scene-asset refusal exporting an existing clip. Make ownership independent of tier. The small fix is personal always user-owned, collaborative always Workspace-owned, plus direct correction/reset of affected development rows. Do not introduce dual lookup.

7. **B07, P2, verified rounding and display contract mismatch.** Docs `content/docs/index.mdx:31` promise rounding each video up to the next minute. `processing-usage.ts:131` rounds only to seconds, `:604` settles those seconds, `:258` sums them, and `:733` rounds the sum. Scenario: sixty 1-second sources use 1 displayed minute, whereas promised per-source rounding would use 60. Separately 1 settled second plus 1 reserved second displays 1 used + 1 reserved + 59 remaining against a 60 limit. Choose per-source whole credits, quantize debit seconds once, and return a sum-consistent integer projection.

8. **B08, P2, verified nonexistent paid deliverable.** `pricing.ts:122` sells generated video; `plan-features.ts:77`, `:96` enable it. `generated-media.ts:621`, `:649`, `generated-media-prisma-store.ts:56` only implement image jobs, and repository search found no video-generation caller. Remove the flag, unused kind branch, marketing bullet, and associated misleading copy in this cutover. Building a new video provider is outside this plan.

9. **B09, P2, verified retention depends on opt-in configuration.** `project-retention.service.ts:55` defaults to observe; `:91` also needs an activation timestamp. `:74` gives Free projects no deadline otherwise. Pricing promises three days unconditionally. Scenario: a newly deployed environment lacks either setting; Free media never gets an expiry. This review did not read live environment values. Remove the old observe/activation cutover path under the pre-production policy, assign canonical Free deadlines unconditionally, and test web and worker expiry together. Backfill/reset development fixtures explicitly.

10. **B10, P2, verified generation allowances are incomplete and not durable to actor deletion.** Dubbing `requestClipDub:198–255` creates/reuses dubs without any count budget; rate limits are per actor and do not supply a monthly allowance. Image limits live at `generated-media-usage-ledger.ts:20` instead of the catalog. `schema.prisma:2276` cascades image usage when the actor User is deleted, while the Workspace is the budget owner. Scenario: a Business member uses image allowance, account deletion removes their usage rows, and the Workspace gains those requests again. Membership removal alone does not delete them. Reuse the reservation pattern for image and dub counts, keep actor nullable with SetNull, and make counts Workspace-owned and period-bound.

### Additional findings that must shape implementation

- **B11, P2, inconsistent brand boundaries.** Free custom templates/logos are allowed by `brand-template.service.ts:207`, `:437`, and applied through `project.service.ts:496`. Profile governance accepts `approval_required` on Creator/Pro at `brand-profile.service.ts:412`, `:461`, although Review Rooms require Business at `review.service.ts:399`. Add custom-template/logo and governed-brand entitlements. A Creator profile must not become unpublishable through a review rule its plan cannot satisfy.
- **B12, P2, downgrade creates a Scene removal trap.** `scene-document-mutation.ts:57–67` includes removed IDs in changedIds and blocks every change on Free. A downgraded user cannot remove paid Scene content to make an export eligible. Allow removal/deactivation and nonpremium repairs; require entitlement only for additions, activation, or premium changes. Fonts/assets/profile deletion and unlinking need the same cleanup rule.
- **B13, P2, defense gap at intake acceptance.** `ProcessingUsage.reserve:329` discards Workspace status, while `settle:575` checks it. A request authorized before a concurrent billing restriction can still reserve/create intake and trigger download before settlement refuses it. Check active status in reserve under the same lock used for access projection changes. This is a stale-admission race, not evidence that ordinary unauthenticated requests can upload.
- **B14, P2, export/editor guards are owned by adapters.** New `ClipExportService.create:563–592` checks asset availability and output limits but not motion entitlement or stale censor segments. Browser `route.ts:1291–1307` supplies those checks; shared integration `createClipExport:300` does not. Today Business-only integrations mask the tier case, but they can already bypass stale-censor export refusal. Move shared validation into the service; do not rely on rollout state or transport gating to make a downstream check unnecessary. Document persistence `:1456` authorizes capability but lacks the web's scene/motion/censor entitlement deltas.
- **B15, P2, product limits have no declared plan owner.** Social connections and scheduling, basic Autopilot/Zoom, custom template/profile/font counts, and Editor Timelines have only role/global safety checks. This is a gap against the proposed model, not a claim that an existing advertised social limit is violated. Add only the boundaries explicitly accepted below.
- **B16, policy limitation, not a discovered charging bug.** A reservation pins its UTC admission month at `reserve:330`; later settlement reads current tier against that old month at `settle:568`. A September upload completed in October belongs to September and may be refused after downgrade. This is already tested and documented. Keep it unless the owner chooses billing-anchor periods. Do not silently label Stripe renewal date as the credit reset date.
- **B17, policy limitation, not a refund bug.** Settled usage intentionally survives STT/detection/render failure, regeneration, deletion, and purge under ADR 0012. Failed/abandoned ingest before settlement releases usage. `refund` excludes settled usage from the original period, records actor/reason, and does not mint current-period allowance. A late operator refund therefore does not refill this month. Show this policy; do not add an automatic post-STT refund that races free retry.
- **B18, policy limitation.** Past-due grace starts on first durable local observation at `workspace-billing.service.ts:2192`, not Stripe invoice creation. The runbook explicitly says so and tests pin it at `workspace-billing.service.test.ts:1277`. Delayed reconciliation can extend economic access after provider delinquency. Retain the documented seven-day observation policy for this change; changing the time origin is a separate owner decision and must handle recovery/new delinquency episodes.
- **B19, consistency risk.** Capacity and generated-image env overrides can make enforcement disagree between web and worker and with static pricing copy. Retain only operational concurrency overrides. Move saleable allowances into the shared catalog and update `env:check` if any remaining shared controls affect access.

Preserve the good invariants: source settlement is locked and idempotent; Project deletion cannot refund it; source objects are pinned before probing; operation receipts replay across REST/MCP; Workspace Billing retrieves current provider state instead of applying event payloads. This review found no reason to replace those systems.

## 3. Proposed product and entitlement model

### Credit semantics

1. `creditsForSourceSeconds(s) = max(1, ceil(s / 60))` for finite positive measured duration. Reject invalid/zero media. Compare actual source seconds to the duration cap before rounding the debit. Examples: 1 sec -> 1 credit; 60 sec -> 1; 60.01 sec -> 2; 30 min -> 30.
2. One Project intake is the unit. Local files, supported links, Drive, RSS, Zoom, and caption-only imports all consume credits. Charge the entire source, even when the selected clipping window is shorter. A new Project from the same source is another intake. No cross-Project content deduplication or hashed-media wallet.
3. Reserve credits in the acceptance transaction, reducing available balance immediately. Metadata can resize an unsettled reservation. Confirm the exact debit from pinned/probed source facts inside first generation admission. Intake finalization and worker retries never debit a second time. The user sees reserved separately from used.
4. Keep storage in `ProcessingUsageReservation.reservedSeconds` and `.settledSeconds`, storing the credit debit as multiples of 60. These fields are debit seconds, not video geometry. The Project/source probe remains the exact duration owner. Add a small conversion helper and database checks; do not store an independent drifting credit balance or a second counter.
5. UTC monthly budget. Period starts at 00:00 UTC on the first; expose `periodStart` and `renewsAt`. An annual plan has the same monthly refresh cadence. No rollover, overdraft, per-clip/render charge, or purchase packs. Source reservations retain their admission period across midnight/month-end; release returns room only to that period. A released intake's explicit manual retry re-admits into the current period.
6. Preserve pre-STT release and audited post-STT refunds from ADR 0012. A retry of the same Project is free; a new import is charged. Operator refund is a source-credit correction, never a Stripe money refund. Explain both separately in billing support copy.
7. Replace `settle`'s missing/released-reservation legacy auto-admission at `processing-usage.ts:518–566` with a strict current-architecture failure. All intakes must have reserved. Manual retry already has an explicit re-admission path. Directly repair/reset development rows; keep no legacy parser, dual accounting, or fallback charge route.
8. A tier upgrade raises the current month's ceiling without resetting used/reserved. A downgrade applies the lower ceiling at its effective time, leaves history intact, and clamps remaining to zero. No new Free bonus after cancel if the month's usage already exceeds 60. A rollover creates a new query period, not a scheduled balance-minting job.
9. The UTC decision is deliberate. A purchase on October 31 can use October's allowance and then November's before Stripe renews. There is no per-renewal credit grant. This is a real commercial tradeoff of retaining ADR 0012, not a ledger arithmetic bug. If the owner requires one paid month per credit bucket, reopen ADR 0012 and implement billing-anchor periods before launch instead. Do not pretend a rename solves that requirement.

Top-ups and rollover are not worth building now. Both require grant lots, expiry ordering, pack-to-subscription access rules, refund allocation, tax/Checkout products, and additional support explanations. Upgrade or wait is enough while there are no production customers. Revisit packs only when denied demand and margin data justify a wallet.

### Upgrade, downgrade, payment, cancel, rollover

- **Upgrade.** Free -> paid uses one guarded Checkout per Workspace. Paid changes use Stripe portal. A checkout-return query parameter is never proof of payment. Reconcile current provider state, then atomically commit paid access and retention rescue. New catalog capabilities apply on refresh without a new API key or OAuth grant. Existing scopes remain the same. Current-month used/reserved credits and image/dub counts stay in place; upgrade raises the ceiling, never adds another full bucket. Versioned old Free exports remain watermarked; a new export can use paid output.
- **Upgrade money.** Require immediate collection/payment-dependent upgrade behavior verified with Stripe test clocks/SCA/failure cases. A mere active status with deferred proration is insufficient evidence for an unpaid increase. If the configured portal cannot provide this guarantee, disable portal upgrades and add one narrow Workspace Billing paid-change operation with an invoice preview and pending update; keep portal payment recovery/cancel. This fallback is an implementation contingency to verify, not permission to build a second billing facade now.
- **Scheduled downgrade.** Recommend at Stripe billing-period end, with a visible effective date; cancellation at period end works the same way. Keep old plan until provider confirms the change. Applying a lower tier never deletes usage or multiplies refunds. Credit period remains UTC, so billing renewal and allowance refresh are distinct dates.
- **At effective downgrade.** New actions use lower features/limits; retained rows remain. Restrict any collaborative Workspace with non-owner members below Business, including Pro/Creator. Owner gets billing/download/member cleanup recovery, not a forced member deletion. With excess accounts/rules, eligibility is deterministic; never revoke tokens automatically. Pause Autopilot/Standing Grants and new event delivery when no longer entitled. A pending post that has not reached first provider submission becomes an actionable plan-ineligible outcome, not an external upload. Already submitted work is reconciled to a known outcome without resubmitting.
- **Accepted work.** Preserve source ledger's current-tier check for unconfirmed intake settlement; a downgrade can refuse an overlong/unaffordable intake and release its reservation before STT. Confirmed source work may finish without a second charge. Versioned exports keep their accepted output settings. Generated image/dub requests with durable count reservations finish under their accepted grant. Finishing remains the existing optional current-entitlement check and can skip. For new exports of retained premium edits, keep the existing motion restriction but enforce it in the service; allow scene/censor/font removal and reuse of already saved snapshots. Do not silently strip accepted branding or censoring.
- **Past due.** Preserve seven days from first durable observation, as currently documented. Retain last verified paid tier during grace, show payment-required banner and deadline, and refuse new billable-member additions. Credits/counts are the ordinary calendar ceiling, not a new grace allowance. After grace, project Free/restricted as appropriate and apply the same lower-tier guards. Paid subscription recovery uses the portal, never another Checkout while that subscription can still bill. Recovery clears grace and restores the mapped paid catalog; usage does not reset.
- **Cancel/end/unpaid/paused/incomplete_expired.** cancelAtPeriodEnd retains current access until provider cancellation takes effect. Terminal verified state commits Free fallback even if seat convergence would fail. Personal returns to active Free; collaborative below-Business restrictions apply. For first incomplete checkout, keep existing Free/pending-payment rules; no paid credit bucket exists until paid access verifies. Completed paid artifacts remain downloadable during retained Project lifetime. Unexpired paid Projects get 28-day post-paid grace; new Free projects get 72 hours.
- **Rollover.** At the UTC boundary, new intake and count reservations use the new month. Existing reservations/finalized rows keep their owning period. No scheduled "reset usage" mutation, Stripe webhook increment, or annual lump sum. Expose the previous-period status on an unfinished operation if the current dashboard no longer shows that reservation. Free image lifetime sample never resets.
- **Provider outage/catalog conflict.** Preserve the last verified projection as ADR 0006 requires. Report stale health and retry due reconciliation; never guess a downgrade from one failed request. Do not mutate/cancel/refund duplicate provider subscriptions automatically. Prevent them at admission and make existing conflicts owner/operator-visible.
