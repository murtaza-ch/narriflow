# Listing copy

**Name:** Narriflow

**Short description (one line):** Turn long recordings into branded, captioned short clips, export them with subtitles, and send them for client approval.

**Long description:**

Narriflow finds the moments worth sharing in a long recording and renders them as captioned short clips in your Brand Profile's style. From your assistant you can upload an original file or submit a link, choose a Brand Profile and clip settings, and follow exactly the work you started until the clips are ready. Review clips by score and timing, export the edit you approved in the formats you need, and fetch the MP4 files and matching SRT or VTT subtitles when you ask for them. When a client needs to sign off, the assistant prepares a Narriflow link with the exports selected; you send the round, and the assistant can report which clips were approved or need changes. Social posts are scheduled only after you confirm the exact post.

The connector works with workspaces on an active Business plan. Processing uses your workspace's minutes.

**Sample prompts:**

1. "Upload this interview to my Acme workspace with the Acme Brand Profile and make 8 vertical clips under a minute."
2. "How is the clip generation I just started going? Which clips failed?"
3. "Export the top three clips as 9:16 and 1:1 in 1080p."
4. "Give me the download links and SRT subtitles for that export."
5. "Send those exports to the client for review." (returns the composer link)
6. "Did the client approve the review round for the Acme interview?"
