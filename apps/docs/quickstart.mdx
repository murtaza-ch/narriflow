---
title: Quickstart
description: Turn a video into clips, review them in Studio, then export or schedule them.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a video">
    Select **New project** in the sidebar, then choose a source.

    - **A file.** Drop a video or audio file (MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, WAV, M4A or AAC), up to 5 GB.
    - **A link.** Paste a link from YouTube, Google Drive, Loom, Vimeo, Dropbox, StreamYard, Twitch, X, TikTok, LinkedIn or Facebook.
    - **A podcast feed.** Paste an RSS URL, then pick an episode.

    Give the project a name if you want one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose settings">
    Pick **AI clipping** to get short clips, or **Caption only** to caption the whole video. For clips, set the Brand Profile, aspect ratio, clip length, caption style and spoken language. To steer the result, describe a moment in **Find a specific moment**.

    Select **Upload & get AI clips**. For a link, Narriflow imports it first, then you select **Get AI clips**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the clips">
    Open the project and stay on the **Clips** tab. Each clip shows its virality score and sub-scores. Sort by virality, hook strength, duration or timeline order. Use the scissors button to trim or extend a clip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit in Studio">
    Select **Edit** on a clip. Studio has tools for captions, Brand template, B-roll, motion, text, music, layout and scenes. Edits save as you work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export or schedule">
    In Studio, open **Export**, choose one or more formats and a resolution (720p or 1080p), then confirm. Exports render in the background and appear on the **Exports** page. Free plan exports are 720p with a Narriflow watermark.

    To post a clip, select **Publish** on it. Choose the accounts, write the description, and either publish now or pick a time. Connect accounts first in **Settings → Social accounts**. Scheduled posts appear on **Calendar** in month, week and list views.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Narriflow publishes to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, LinkedIn and X. For TikTok you can publish directly or send the video to your TikTok inbox and finish there.
