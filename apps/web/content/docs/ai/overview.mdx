## What the assistant can do

  <Card title="Find your work" icon="folder-search">
    List your Workspaces, Projects, Brand Profiles and clips ranked by virality. Read a clip's timing, scores and edit version. Check your credits.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Create clips" icon="scissors">
    Submit a video link or upload a file, pick a Brand Profile and generation settings, generate clips again for a ready Project, and follow the work until it finishes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Deliver" icon="package-check">
    Export a reviewed clip in the formats you choose, get download links and subtitles on request, and prepare a client review link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Publish" icon="send">
    List connected accounts, prepare an exact post, schedule it after you confirm, recover a failed post, and manage RSS Autopilot rules.
  </Card>

See [Tools](/docs/ai/tools) for all 35 tools.

## Who can use it

- **Pro and Business Workspaces.** The Workspace must be active and on the Pro or Business plan. Other plans cannot use the connector.
- **Your role applies.** The assistant acts as you, in any Workspace where your role allows the action. Viewers can read but cannot start work. See [Permissions](/docs/ai/permissions).
- **Credits.** Importing and uploading a video use the Workspace's credits, the same as in the app: one credit per started minute of the video. Generating clips again for the same Project is free.

## How it works

The assistant reads by default and writes only on clear intent. Write tools start processing or change publishing, so each needs a write permission that you approve when you connect. Scheduling a post adds a second check. Narriflow shows the exact post, and nothing is scheduled until you confirm it.

Tool results contain product facts and Narriflow links, not media URLs or transcripts. Download links are issued only when you ask for files, and they expire after 15 minutes.
