---
title: Introduction
description: Narriflow turns long videos into short, captioned clips, scores each one, and helps you edit, export and publish them.
---

Add a video file, a video link or a podcast feed. Narriflow finds the strongest moments, cuts them into clips and adds captions. Each clip gets a virality score with sub-scores for hook, emotional intensity, story completeness, pacing and duration. You edit clips in Studio, export them in the formats you need, and publish or schedule them to your social accounts.

The assistant connector and the API require a Workspace on an active Business plan.

## Terms used in these docs

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Workspace | The account that owns projects, members, Brand Profiles, connected social accounts and the plan. |
| Project | One source video and everything generated from it, including its clips. |
| Clip | A short segment Narriflow cut from a Project, with its own captions, scores and edits. |
| Brand Profile | Reusable branding for a Workspace: logo, fonts, caption styles and writing guidance. |
| Recipe | A named set of settings: a Brand Profile, language, clip settings and finishing. Autopilot rules, the API and your assistants use one by name. |
| Processing minutes | Your monthly allowance. Each video counts as its source length, rounded up to the next minute. The allowance resets every UTC month. |
| Upload Session | The record of one file upload. It tracks the transfer until Narriflow starts processing the file. |
